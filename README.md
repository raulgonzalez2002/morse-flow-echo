# EchoWeaver: The Rhythmic Language Forge 🧠

Welcome to **EchoWeaver**, an open-source cognitive playground that transforms the ancient art of Morse code into a modern, interactive learning experience. While the original project focuses on basic repetition, EchoWeaver is a complete re-imagining—a **full-spectrum auditory training ecosystem** designed for the 2026 learner who craves depth, personalization, and genuine mastery.

This is not just another flashcard app. EchoWeaver is a **sonic gymnasium** where your brain builds new neural pathways through adaptive pacing, gamified progression, and real-time biofeedback. Think of it as a personal coach that lives in your browser, whispering the language of dots and dashes into your ear until it becomes second nature.

---

## 🌟 Why EchoWeaver? The Philosophy of "Signal Over Noise"

Most learning tools give you content. EchoWeaver gives you **a journey**. We believe that Morse code isn't just about communication—it's a **meditative discipline** that sharpens focus, memory, and pattern recognition. Here’s what sets us apart:

- **Adaptive Difficulty Engine** 🔄: Unlike static lessons, our engine analyzes your reaction times (to the millisecond) and error patterns. It then dynamically adjusts the waveform speed, frequency, and character grouping to keep you in the "flow state"—not bored, not overwhelmed.
- **The "Rhythmic Recall" Method** 🥁: Instead of rote memorization, we use a proprietary spaced-repetition algorithm intertwined with musical tempo. You learn to *feel* the code as a rhythm, not just hear it as a sound.
- **Multi-Sensory Feedback Loop** 🎨: Visual waveform visualization, haptic-like visual pulses, and gamified score multipliers create a rich feedback environment that rewards precision and speed.
- **A Community of Craft** 🤝: Practice modes, global leaderboards, and shareable "sound signatures" allow you to compare your journey with others, without the pressure of social media noise.

## 🚀 Key Features: More Than Meets the Ear

### 1. 🧩 The Forge Mode (Adaptive Practice)
- **Progressive Character Tree**: Start with single letters, then branch into numbers, punctuation, and prosigns. The system unlocks new characters only when you demonstrate 90% accuracy on the current set.
- **Variable Bandwidth Slider**: Adjust frequency from 200Hz to 900Hz to mimic real-world radio conditions. Practice with background noise filters (static, rain, cockpit hum) to build resilience.
- **Echo Sessions**: A "shadow mode" where you hear a phrase, then type it back within a strict time window. The AI evaluates your cadence, not just correctness.

### 2. 📊 The Cognitive Dashboard (Analytics)
- **Brain Wave Heatmap**: A visual representation of your learning activity per character, showing which sounds your brain confuses (e.g., `D` vs `B`).
- **Reaction Latency Graphs**: Track your P50 and P90 response times to identify moments of hesitation.
- **Streak & Flow Score**: Not just a number—a composite metric of speed, accuracy, and consistency. The "Flow Score" is our unique alternative to simple completion percentages.

### 3. 🌐 Multilingual Support & International Phonetics
- Full support for Latin, Cyrillic, Arabic, and Greek Morse alphabets.
- Localized interface in 12+ languages, including Spanish, Hindi, Mandarin, and German. The learning content adapts to your native phonetic base to reduce confusion.

### 4. 🎮 Gamified Progression (The "Arcade" of Sound)
- **Daily Quests**: "Decode 5 words without error on 'Lightning' speed" or "Identify 3 'Q' codes in a row."
- **Milestone Badges**: Awarded for consistent practice streaks, speed milestones (WPM), and perfect accuracy runs.
- **The Ascension Ladder**: A virtual rank system from "Novice Listener" to "Cosmic Radioman," visually represented through a beautiful progress nebula.

### 5. ⚡ Zero-Friction Accessibility
- **Responsive UI**: Whether you're on a 4K monitor or a small mobile screen, the interface adapts fluidly. Touch-optimized controls for tablets.
- **Dark/Light Theme**: Eye-strain prevention for late-night practice sessions.
- **Keyboard-First Navigation**: For power users who prefer full control without a mouse.

## 🛠 Technology Stack (The Engine Room)

- **Frontend**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES2026+) with a modular component architecture. No bloated frameworks—just fast, lightweight DOM manipulation.
- **State Management**: A custom reactive store pattern for real-time UI updates.
- **Audio Synthesis**: Web Audio API generates pure sine waves with adjustable envelope (attack/decay) to simulate classic oscillators.
- **Data Persistence**: LocalStorage for offline progress tracking, with optional export/import of your learning profile as a JSON "soundprint."
- **Service Worker**: Enables fully offline functionality—practice in the subway, on a mountain, or anywhere else.

## 📦 Getting Started (Your First Session)

1. **Access**: Simply open the deployed web app in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). No account creation is required for the core experience.
2. **Orientation**: The onboarding wizard will run a 30-second "hearing calibration" to set your baseline sensitivity.
3. **Choose Your Path**: Select "Pure Beginner," "Former Learner Reboot," or "Advanced Refresher."
4. **First Forge**: You'll immediately hear your first character, `E` (a single dot), at a comfortable 15 WPM. Follow the on-screen prompt to type it.
5. **Post-Session Review**: After 5 minutes, the dashboard will show your Flow Score and suggest next steps.
